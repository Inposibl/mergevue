## 4. DEFINITION OF FINAL CALIBRATED REPLAY

For the purpose of public historical case studies, an analytical result qualifies as Final Calibrated Replay ONLY when ALL of the following conditions are simultaneously and strictly met:

A. **Methodological Closure:** The mandatory historical calibration program has reached the relevant accepted methodological closure.
B. **Method Freeze:** The applicable methodology generation has been frozen through an accepted Method Freeze decision.
C. **Final Methodology Replay:** The historical case is replayed using that final accepted methodology.
D. **Sealed Pre-T0 Evidence Set:** The replay operates strictly on the case's original, sealed pre-T0 evidence set.
E. **No Post-T0 Contamination:** No post-T0 outcome evidence is allowed to change the analytical inputs or pre-T0 determination/hypothesis process.
F. **Independent Verification:** The replay result has passed required independent verification.
G. **Owner Acceptance:** The replay result has received explicit Owner acceptance for publication authority.
H. **Public Source Provenance:** Its public-source provenance is complete enough to support the public claims being shown.

A result that fails any required condition above is NOT Public Case Study Authority.

## 5. HISTORICAL-RECORD PRESERVATION AND NO RETROSPECTIVE REWRITING

Pre-calibration artifacts remain immutable internal history.

This includes, but is not limited to:
- original pre-T0 analysis;
- early hypotheses;
- blocked or unresolved states;
- prediction candidates;
- prediction seals;
- correction acts;
- verification acts;
- methodology debts;
- calibration deltas.

Internal historical records MUST NOT be rewritten, elided, or altered to pretend that the final calibrated result existed earlier in the development lifecycle.

The final calibrated replay becomes the PUBLIC authority.
The historical development chain remains INTERNAL evidence.

Therefore:
- **PUBLIC SELECTION changes.**
- **HISTORICAL RECORD does not.**

## 6. PUBLIC WEBSITE SURFACE

The public website must expose ONLY the final calibrated public case-study authority.

The allowed public analytical surface consists strictly of:
- the final calibrated result;
- accepted public Environment terminology;
- business-language explanation of the final result;
- key causal and business mechanisms to the extent approved for public disclosure;
- final confidence and limitations where materially necessary;
- public source links supporting the factual basis of the answer;
- outcome comparison after lawful outcome reveal, clearly and cleanly separated from the pre-T0 analytical basis.

The public website is a diagnostic product and public case-study resource; it is NOT a methodology-development log or an internal debate transcript.

---

## 7. PUBLIC-SOURCE TRACEABILITY RULE

Every publicly presented historical case study must expose the public sources from which the published factual and analytical answer was derived.

The public source layer must preserve, to the extent available:
- source title;
- publisher or originating institution;
- publication date;
- public URL;
- sufficient locator information (e.g. section, page, or timestamp) to make the citation auditable.

Primary or nearest-to-primary public sources must always be preferred. An internal MergeVue summary must NEVER be substituted for the underlying public source when that underlying public source is available.

---

## 8. PRE-T0 VS POST-T0 SOURCE SEPARATION

If a published case includes the historical outcome, the public case presentation MUST strictly separate:

A. **ANALYTICAL BASIS SOURCES**
from:
B. **OUTCOME VERIFICATION SOURCES**.

- **ANALYTICAL BASIS SOURCES** are the public sources lawfully available to the sealed pre-T0 replay that establish the pre-deal evidence and analytical inputs.
- **OUTCOME VERIFICATION SOURCES** may be post-T0 public sources and may be shown solely and strictly as evidence of what actually occurred post-deal.

Outcome-verification sources MUST NOT be represented, directly or indirectly, as having contributed to or informed the pre-T0 analytical answer. This separation is mandatory and non-negotiable.

---

## 9. INTERNAL-ONLY METHODOLOGY-DEVELOPMENT MATERIAL

The ordinary public case-study experience must NOT expose or rely upon superseded internal methodology-development records as the authoritative answer.

Unless separately and explicitly approved for a controlled investor or audit context, the public surface must exclude:
- pre-calibration analytical outputs;
- superseded conclusions;
- intermediate hypothesis rankings;
- H-D1 / H-D2 / H-D3-style internal working labels;
- T10 development status;
- MD-1 through MD-N methodology-debt registers;
- correction chains;
- auditor chains;
- agent identities;
- validator history;
- rejected candidate rules;
- calibration-development chronology;
- proprietary internal theory terminology;
- internal Environment codes where those codes are not public terminology;
- `Phenomenon / Essence` or equivalent closed internal theory language;
- internal evidence-room filesystem and provenance machinery.

This firewall does NOT permit destroying or purging such records; all historical records remain permanently preserved internally.

## 10. INVESTOR / AUDIT EVIDENCE ROOM SEPARATION

The public website layer and the internal evidence layer are distinct disclosure surfaces:
- The complete methodological history, calibration logs, and audit chains MAY remain accessible under separately governed investor due diligence, independent audit, controlled technical review, or internal falsification/calibration review.
- The availability of historical records in an internal evidence room does NOT make that history public case-study authority.
- No investor portal or disclosure route is implemented by this governance document; this document establishes governance boundaries only.

## 12. HARD GATE — WEBSITE CASE-STUDY ROUTE

Any public website case-study route, including the route family rooted at `/case-studies/` (e.g. `/case-studies/:caseId` registered in `src/screenRegistry.js`), must eventually enforce a fail-closed publication gate.

Future website runtime requirements:
- The route must NOT render a provisional, pre-calibration, superseded, or non-final analytical object as the authoritative public historical case result.
- If Final Calibrated Replay authority does not exist for the requested case, the route must NOT silently fall back to an earlier candidate or provisional hypothesis.
- Exact runtime, UI components, and schema implementations are intentionally deferred to the website case-study implementation act.

This document does NOT claim that the website runtime gate is already implemented in code; it establishes the governance invariant that future implementation must enforce.

---

## 13. HARD GATE — 10-CASE PUBLICATION PIPELINE

The `10-case publication pipeline` (operationalizing the `FINAL CONSISTENT 10-CASE CORPUS` and `10-case consistency replay` requirements of Control Tree v2.1 Section 5.9) MUST operate only AFTER:
- the final accepted methodology generation is frozen;
- all materially affected historical cases are replayed under the frozen methodology;
- the same final methodology-generation rules are consistently applied across the published corpus;
- each public case has achieved a valid Final Calibrated Replay;
- required independent verification is complete;
- required Owner acceptance is complete;
- public source traceability is complete.

Only the Final Calibrated Replay output may feed generation of public case-study artifacts. Historical and pre-calibration deltas remain internally documented but are never substituted into public outputs.

This document does NOT claim that the 10-case calibration program or publication pipeline is already complete; it establishes the hard gating requirements for pipeline execution.

---

## 14. FAIL-CLOSED PUBLICATION RULE

The publication system must strictly fail closed.

If a case does not yet possess a valid Final Calibrated Replay with required independent verification, Owner acceptance, and public source traceability, then that case has:

`NO AUTHORITATIVE PUBLIC CASE-STUDY RESULT YET`

Under no circumstances may the system:
- publish the latest available provisional result;
- publish the strongest old hypothesis;
- publish an analyst-selected fallback;
- infer an analytical result from the known outcome;
- substitute post-T0 knowledge into pre-T0 analysis;
- silently expose an internal candidate.

---

## 15. PRESERVATION OF FINAL UNRESOLVED / INSUFFICIENT RESULTS

Calibration discipline must not create pressure to force every case into an artificial determination.

If the final calibrated replay lawfully results in:
- `UNRESOLVED`;
- `INSUFFICIENT`;
- pair unavailable / blocked;
- or another accepted methodological uncertainty state;

that uncertainty is itself the authoritative final calibrated result. The public layer must never fabricate certainty or suppress legitimate indeterminacy. Whether an unresolved case is selected for public marketing display is a separate publication-selection decision; but if published, only its calibrated state of uncertainty is authoritative.

---

## 16. PUBLIC TERMINOLOGY FIREWALL

Public historical case studies must adhere to the controlling proprietary-language rules:
- Allowed public vocabulary: accepted public names of the nine Interaction Environments, ordinary business language, and approved resource/friction/integration concepts.
- Closed internal theory vocabulary is strictly prohibited from public presentation. Specifically, `Phenomenon / Essence` and equivalent closed internal theory terminology remain INTERNAL ONLY.
- Internal environment codes (e.g. `NF/NT`, `NT/STJ`, `STP/STJ`) remain internal working shorthand and must not be exposed to the public unless an explicit Owner decision establishes a public alias or authorization.
