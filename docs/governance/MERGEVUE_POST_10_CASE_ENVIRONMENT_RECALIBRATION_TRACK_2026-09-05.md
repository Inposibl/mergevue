---

## 0. PURPOSE

This document freezes the Owner-accepted roadmap for how MergeVue must re-verify, recalibrate, and ultimately determine historical Environments after all ten fixed corpus members have completed their blind analytical cycles.

The purpose is **not** to look at outcomes and retroactively assign Environment labels.

The purpose is to establish whether a historical Environment-determination route can be made:

1. factually grounded;
2. reproducible across independent analysts;
3. behaviorally valid against later observed mechanisms;
4. resistant to hindsight;
5. resistant to overfitting;
6. explicit about indeterminacy.

The project must not force all historical organizational sides into a determinate Environment result.

`NOT DETERMINABLE` is a valid final state.

---

The twenty sealed factual baselines are then transformed into a common mechanism representation.

---

## 8. INDEPENDENT DUAL CODING

At least two independent analytical actors should code the same sealed pre-T0 facts into the same mechanism schema.

They must not receive each other’s conclusions.

The purpose is to test whether the observational layer is itself reproducible.

Disagreement is material evidence.

Example:

Analyst A:
`centralized authority`

Analyst B:
`formal corporate oversight with substantial operating autonomy`

This is not dismissed as model noise.

It means the mechanism representation is not yet sufficiently explicit.

Before Environment determination, unresolved mechanism-level ambiguity must be preserved or resolved through admissible pre-T0 evidence.

Outcome evidence may not be used to resolve it.

## 9. HISTORICAL ENVIRONMENT DETERMINATION CONTRACT

Only after factual and mechanism layers are stable may a candidate historical Environment-determination rule be formulated.

The Owner-accepted target route is:

`sealed documentary facts`
→
`observed mechanisms`
→
`scoped organizational domain`
→
`theory-native Environment signatures`
→
`supporting evidence`
→
`counterevidence`
→
`candidate Environment`
→
`strongest alternative`
→
`falsifier`
→
`confidence / NOT DETERMINABLE`

This route is theory-native.

It is not constitutionally dependent on an exact questionnaire anchor for every historical conclusion.

But it must not become:

`fact → code`

or:

`resource → code`

or:

`synthetic questionnaire answers`

The questionnaire remains the strongest direct-measurement route for live respondent use.

Historical inference is a separate route and must remain explicitly distinguished from direct respondent measurement.

---

## 10. DETERMINATION STANDARD

A historical Environment may be determined only when:

1. constitutive mechanisms are materially supported;
2. the candidate Environment explains the observed mechanism set better than the strongest alternative;
3. material counterevidence has been considered;
4. no critical falsifier is present;
5. scope is explicitly defined;
6. confidence is justified by evidence;
7. the result is reproducible under the same rule.

A determination must not be made by simple feature counting.

Forbidden simplification:

`5 matching signals → Environment X`

The determination rule must preserve:

- supporting evidence;
- counterevidence;
- strongest alternative;
- falsifier;
- uncertainty;
- indeterminacy.

---

## 11. VALID FINAL STATES FOR ONE ORGANIZATIONAL SIDE

A side may end as:

### DETERMINATE

Evidence supports one Environment strongly enough under the frozen rule.

### HYPOTHESIS WITH MATERIAL ALTERNATIVE

Evidence supports one Environment more strongly, but a serious alternative remains.

### NOT DETERMINABLE

Evidence is insufficient, contradictory, too weak, or too incomplete to support a lawful Environment determination.

The project must not force determinate labels merely to achieve full corpus coverage.

## 14. INDEPENDENT REPRODUCTION

A separate eligible actor must reproduce the entire 20-side replay independently.

The independent actor receives:

- the same sealed factual corpus;
- the same mechanism representation;
- the same frozen determination contract.

The actor must not receive the first actor’s final labels as guidance.

Required comparison:

- Environment result;
- strongest alternative;
- falsifier;
- confidence;
- NOT DETERMINABLE state;
- scope.

Material disagreement is not treated as harmless LLM variance.

It indicates that the rule or representation remains insufficiently deterministic.

Environment outputs must not proceed to ECS until reproducibility is acceptable.

---

## 15. OUTCOME-BASED BEHAVIORAL VALIDATION

Only after Prediction Seal and outcome reconstruction may later behavior be used to test the historical determination.

Outcome does not become:

`ground-truth Environment label`

Instead, it tests the pre-outcome hypothesis.

Questions include:

- Did predicted mechanisms appear?
- Did the expected authority pattern manifest?
- Did a declared falsifier appear?
- Did the strongest alternative explain the later behavior better?
- Was the original determination overconfident?
- Should the pre-outcome result have been NOT DETERMINABLE?

This preserves anti-hindsight discipline.

## 17. OVERFITTING CONTROL — LEAVE-ONE-CASE-OUT

The corpus contains only ten transactions.

Therefore empirically tuned parameters must be tested for stability.

For any parameter, threshold, confidence rule, or empirical narrowing rule that is learned or tuned from the corpus, perform leave-one-case-out testing:

For each case:

`9 cases → calibration`

then:

`1 excluded case → test`

Repeat for all ten cases.

Evaluate:

- result stability;
- threshold stability;
- confidence stability;
- falsifier stability;
- alternative-ranking stability;
- NOT DETERMINABLE frequency;
- sensitivity to single-case removal.

If removal of one case materially changes the rule, the rule is unstable and must not be presented as robust.

Purely theoretical constitutive rules are not “trained” on the nine cases, but their performance should still be tested on held-out cases.

## 25. THREE REQUIRED VALIDITY LAYERS

A historically determined Environment is not considered robust unless three different validity layers are satisfied.

### LAYER 1 — FACTUAL TRUTH

Are the pre-T0 facts themselves established from lawful evidence?

Questions:

- Was the source public before T0?
- Is provenance valid?
- Is the proposition atomic?
- Is temporal state correct?
- Is counterevidence preserved?

### LAYER 2 — CLASSIFICATION REPRODUCIBILITY

Do independent actors using the same sealed facts and same frozen rule obtain the same Environment determination?

Questions:

- Is the mechanism coding reproducible?
- Is the strongest alternative reproducible?
- Is the falsifier reproducible?
- Is the confidence result reproducible?
- Is NOT DETERMINABLE reproducible?

### LAYER 3 — BEHAVIORAL VALIDITY

Does the pre-outcome Environment hypothesis predict or explain later observed mechanisms better than alternatives, without hindsight?

Questions:

- Were expected mechanisms observed?
- Was a falsifier observed?
- Did the strongest alternative outperform the chosen Environment?
- Was confidence calibrated?

Only the intersection of these three layers supports a strong claim of historically valid Environment determination.

## 30. OWNER-ACCEPTED CONTROLLING SUMMARY

The ten-case corpus exists to test whether MergeVue can determine historical Environments from evidence that was actually available before each transaction became public.

The project will therefore:

1. seal and independently verify the factual past;
2. normalize that past into outcome-hidden observed mechanisms;
3. require independent agreement on the observational layer;
4. build an explicit historical determination rule;
5. freeze the rule before replay;
6. replay all twenty organizational sides under one rule;
7. independently reproduce the result;
8. use later outcomes only to falsify or behaviorally validate pre-outcome hypotheses;
9. test empirical tuning for leave-one-case-out stability;
10. preserve the frozen predeclared coverage map and compare it with realized coverage;
11. issue separate verdicts for T10 and MD-1…MD-6;
12. require Owner acceptance of the final determination contract;
13. run a Final Calibrated Replay across all ten cases;
14. independently verify that full replay;
15. only then allow final Environment results to feed pair / ECS / friction and public historical case studies.

The governing standard is not:

> Did the model produce an Environment for every case?

The governing standard is:

> Were the underlying facts true, was the classification reproducible, and did the pre-outcome hypothesis survive later behavioral falsification?

If the answer is incomplete for some cases or some Environments, the project preserves that uncertainty rather than inventing certainty.
