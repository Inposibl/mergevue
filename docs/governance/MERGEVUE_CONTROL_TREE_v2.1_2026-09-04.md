# 17. CONTROL INVARIANTS

## Invariant 1 — Historical corpus

10 historical cases are a minimum falsification corpus, not automatic proof of complete calibration.

## Invariant 2 — Agents vs production Core

The analytical method discovered through agents must ultimately be hard-coded into deterministic Core.

## Invariant 3 — No LLM analytical authority inside Core

LLM may collect candidate evidence and may communicate sealed outputs. It does not own analytical meaning inside production Core.

## Invariant 4 — Two Paid LLM boundaries

Before Core:

`Gemini research → Grok independent re-retrieval / audit`

After Core:

`sealed analytical authority → Writer → Verifier`

## Invariant 5 — Analyst agenda is system-generated

Human analyst work is driven by Evidence Gap Maps and Core-native objects, not an empty-page free-form process.

## Invariant 6 — Analyst UI and Core share the same schema

Analyst UI derives from the same canonical schema and identifiers consumed by Core. Human users do not type canonical IDs manually.

## Invariant 7 — Paid product may lawfully stop short of prediction

Terminal result may be:
- FULL PREDICTIVE DIAGNOSTIC;
- CONDITIONAL SCENARIO DIAGNOSTIC;
- EVIDENCE-LIMITED DIAGNOSTIC.

## Invariant 8 — No commercial incentive to overstate epistemic result

Pricing / compensation / sales process must not reward conversion of an evidence-limited or conditional result into a FULL result.

## Invariant 9 — Case selection precedes pilot outcome reveal

The protocol for selecting Cases 4–10 must be frozen before CASE-3.5 outcome reveal.

## Invariant 10 — Coverage limitations are declared before corpus interpretation

Documentary blind spots and expected low-observability Environment × role cells must be declared before Cases 4–10 are run.

## Invariant 11 — Published case-study boundary is predetermined

What can be disclosed publicly must be decided before the first case is finally closed, so publication is not reverse-designed around favorable results and does not unnecessarily reveal proprietary discriminators or enable questionnaire gaming.

## Invariant 12 — Monitor binds to sealed prediction by digest

Integration Monitor evaluates the exact digitally sealed prediction package, not a later paraphrase of what was supposedly predicted.

# 19. ANTI-DRIFT RULE

Any future act must do one of the following:

1. move an existing node from  
   `❌ → ⚠️ → ☑️ → ✅`;

2. correct a proven defect in an existing node;

3. add a genuinely missing node only after explaining:
   - why the existing tree cannot represent it;
   - what causal/product dependency requires it;
   - why it is not a duplicate of an existing entity;
   - which existing node owns the new dependency.

No parallel methodology, duplicate design system, hidden classifier, extra LLM authority, or free-form analyst pathway may be introduced merely because an agent finds it convenient.
