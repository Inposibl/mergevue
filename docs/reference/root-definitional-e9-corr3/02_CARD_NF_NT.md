# Owner-Accepted Environment Card 1 — NF/NT

**Status:** Owner-accepted semantics preserved in the CORR2 frozen set. Not independently verified or governance-bound by this act.

## 1. Whole-Environment description

`NF/NT` is an environment of inquiry, learning, and creation of theoretical knowledge. Its outward form presents activity as serving a higher mission or ultimate human significance. Its inner operation is continuous truth-seeking, discovery, and conceptual development. `[OWNER-NFNT] [C-11; СТ-6, ¶50] [C-30; СТ-7, ¶608] [NF_NT_REF, Overview!C7:C9]`

## 2. Core logic

Participants generate, exchange, criticise, and develop ideas through rigorous reasoning. When the interaction uses logic and shared rules of argument, the most precise and well-supported position can prevail over title, wealth, tenure, or force. `[OWNER-NFNT] [C-40; СТ-7, ¶630] [NF_NT_REF, Overview!C15:C17]`

## 3. Resource relation

Theoretical knowledge is the primary and, in the Owner's qualified sense, the only legitimate resource of `NF/NT`. It is universal and limitless because the Environment can generate new conceptual resources rather than only redistribute a finite stock. `[OWNER-NFNT] [C-41; СТ-7, ¶632] [NF_NT_REF, Overview!C9,C39:C43]`

Canonical structural shorthand: **Idea → Faith → Knowledge → Power**. Faith is commitment to an unproven idea; Knowledge is an idea developed into a theoretical construction with explanatory power; Power is epistemic authority from demonstrably correct understanding. This is structural logic, not a rigid temporal sequence. `[OWNER-NFNT] [v1.7/03 §2, line 35] [NF_NT_REF, Overview!C40]`

## 4. Power / control

Authority is epistemic. It follows the quality of demonstrated understanding, not coercive or administrative position. `NF/NT` generates knowledge but does not necessarily control its application; `NF/SFJ` and `NT/STJ` may determine how it is applied. `[OWNER-NFNT] [C-40] [NF_NT_REF, Overview!C15,C18]`

## 5. Phenomenon — outward appearance

The NF outward form presents the activity as serving a higher mission or ultimate human significance. This is a genuine phenomenological representation, not a fake promotional shell. No unsupported catalogue of higher meanings is added. `[OWNER-NFNT] [C-11; X4] [NF_NT_REF, Overview!C7]`

## 6. Essence — governing internal structure

The NT inner essence is continuous search for truth, production of new theoretical knowledge, and discovery through rational-analytical work. `[OWNER-NFNT] [C-11; X4] [NF_NT_REF, Overview!C7,C17]`

## 7. Necessary features

At actor level, participants need sufficient autonomous worldview-stage thinking and the capacity to access and switch functionally among theoretical, specialised, and spiritual modes when the interaction requires it. They need not occupy all three modes at once and need not cycle mechanically. `[OWNER-NFNT] [C-10; СТ-6, ¶49] [C-12; СТ-6, ¶51]`

## 8. Typical but not required features

Academic research, teaching, discovery, and knowledge development are common examples, not exclusive markers. Institutional settings may host `NF/NT` work but are not themselves sufficient evidence. `[C-30] [NF_NT_REF, Overview!C29]`

## 9. Nearest-Environment distinction

Unlike `NT/STJ`, knowledge is pursued for truth rather than competitive deployment. Unlike `NF/SFJ`, meaning remains open to evidence and revision rather than fixed by a controlling hierarchy. `[OWNER-NFNT] [NF_NT_REF, Overview!C33:C34]`

## 10. Actor-level conditions

Worldview-stage autonomy is the developmental prerequisite. Functional switching among theoretical, specialised, and spiritual modes is the operational requirement. These are separate axes and must not be reversed into simple classification criteria. `[OWNER-NFNT] [C-10] [C-12]`

## 11. Environment → actor effects

No additional Environment-specific effect is adopted beyond the Owner-accepted reordering of work around inquiry and knowledge creation.

## 12. What must NOT be used as a criterion

Do not classify from an academic label, declared higher purpose, credentials, one successful argument, or an asserted intellectual level. Do not interpret Power as coercive authority or require participants to occupy all three consciousness modes simultaneously. `[OWNER-NFNT]`
