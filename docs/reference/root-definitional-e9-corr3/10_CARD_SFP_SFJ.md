# Owner-Accepted Environment Card 9 — SFP/SFJ

**Status:** Owner-supplied nine-field card integrated in the CORR2 frozen set. Not independently verified or governance-bound by this act.

**Direct semantic authority:** `FINAL_OWNER_AUTHORITY_RECORD_E9_CORR2.md` §9.

## 1. CORE LOGIC

`SFP/SFJ` is an Environment of managed emotional-sensory engagement that converts participant desire into a repeatable resource flow for the internal system.

Outward SFP layer:
- pleasure;
- emotional involvement;
- attractive lifestyle;
- consumption;
- perceived freedom of choice;
- immediate desired experience.

Internal SFJ layer:
- roles;
- hierarchy/control;
- rules;
- repeated practices;
- organised forms of participation.

Structural flow:
`desire / pleasure → voluntary engagement → organised retention and repetition → sustained resource flow into the system`

Governing rule:
participant experiences freedom to pursue emotional/sensory desires, while internal social structure organises available forms of satisfaction so that participation is reproduced and supports the system.

MLM may be an example, never the definition.

## 2. RESOURCE RELATION

Participants may contribute:
- labour;
- time;
- money through consumption;
- attention;
- energy;
- emotional involvement;
- data;
- social contacts;
- reputation;
- recruitment;
- emotional labour.

System may return:
- pleasure;
- convenience;
- product/service;
- emotional stimulation;
- belonging;
- status;
- security;
- income;
- lifestyle access.

Core asymmetry:
**participant experiences the exchange as voluntary satisfaction, while the internal structure is organised to retain more usable resource value than it returns.**

Internal SFJ core:
`organise participation → standardise behaviour → minimise cost of participant contribution → retain/redistribute surplus upward`

Surplus is broader than profit; includes controllable flows of labour, time, attention, emotional energy and repeated consumption.

### Underpaid labour
Do NOT define as illegal below-minimum pay.

Use:
**undercompensated labour** = labour whose compensation is kept low relative to usable value, time and energy extracted.

One extraction channel only, not full Resource Relation.

### “We are family” / appearance of care
NOT necessary.
One possible SFP-facing engagement frame.

Other frames:
fun, pleasure, lifestyle, status, convenience, freedom, personal choice, community, recognition, “you deserve it,” financial opportunity.

Required general structure:
participant experiences resource surrender as desirable, voluntary, rewarding or personally meaningful rather than primarily as imposed extraction.

### v1.7 formula
Old formula:
`extracting resources under appearance of care (“we are family”) → underpaid labour`

Treat as a valid **bounded subtype**, but too narrow for current semantics.

Current broad formula:
`engineered desire / emotional engagement → voluntary resource contribution → controlled repetition → surplus retained by internal system`

## 3. POWER / CONTROL

Real power belongs to actors controlling the **architecture of participation**:
- rules;
- roles;
- metrics;
- access to resources;
- information;
- conditions for continued participation.

Usually owners/organisers/senior management or functional equivalent such as platform-controlled hierarchy.

Four linked mechanisms:
1. hierarchy/procedural control;
2. control of resources/access;
3. information asymmetry may strengthen control but is NOT necessary;
4. participant replaceability may reduce bargaining power but is NOT necessary.

Emotional influence makes participation attractive/acceptable; it is not the primary source of internal authority.

Core asymmetry:
`freedom to choose within the system ≠ power to negotiate the system itself`

Participants may choose products/activities/roles/forms of self-expression, while controlling structure defines available choices, terms of exchange, surplus distribution and continued access.

If participant stops producing value/following required script/maintaining useful engagement, system may:
- reduce access;
- lower role;
- re-engage through rewards/status/recognition/products/promises;
- replace participant if retention cost exceeds value.

Registry:
`power = control over the architecture of participation`

Compact:
`the participant may choose within the system; the controlling group decides what can be chosen, on what terms, and who remains worth keeping`

## 4. NECESSARY FEATURES

Exactly five necessary features:

1. **Sensory / emotional desire**
   Participation activated through pleasure, comfort, attraction, excitement, recognition, lifestyle or another immediate desired experience.

2. **Subjectively voluntary engagement**
   Participant experiences participation mainly as wanted/chosen/enjoyable/attractive, not primarily as direct coercion.
   Full objective freedom is NOT required.

3. **Internal structured control**
   Internal structure controls roles, forms of participation, rules and terms of exchange.
   Formal bureaucracy is NOT required, but controlling SFJ core is.

4. **Reproduction of engagement**
   System reproduces participation over time, through retention or replacement.
   One attractive transaction is insufficient.

5. **Asymmetric resource retention**
   Participants contribute resources while controlling system retains a disproportionately large share of usable value.

Minimum chain:
`sensory/emotional desire → subjectively voluntary participation → internally controlled participation → reproducible engagement → asymmetric resource retention`

Not necessary:
- participant replaceability;
- information asymmetry;
- formal hierarchy;
- minimal compensation;
- undercompensated labour;
- “we are family” rhetoric;
- high turnover.

Structural relevance only if feature contributes to:
`desire → voluntary engagement → structured repetition → asymmetric resource retention`

## 6. DIFFERENCE FROM NEAREST ENVIRONMENTS

### vs SFJ/SFP
Repeated resource flow vs preservation.

SFP/SFJ:
`desire → engagement → repetition → resource flow`

SFJ/SFP:
`trusted relationships → preservation → continuity`

If main mechanism re-engages participant so they contribute again → SFP/SFJ.
If main mechanism preserves existing resources in trusted network → SFJ/SFP.

### vs STP/STJ
Desire vs tactical effectiveness.

SFP/SFJ:
freedom/choice serves desire and engagement.

STP/STJ:
discretion serves tactical adaptation and effectiveness.

### vs NF/SFP
Repeatable participation vs authentic meaning.

SFP/SFJ:
`emotion → repeatable participation`

NF/SFP:
`emotion → authentic meaning / creative expression`

Compact:
- SFP/SFJ = generates repeated participation from desire.
- SFJ/SFP = preserves continuity through trusted relationships.
- STP/STJ = converts flexibility into tactical effectiveness.
- NF/SFP = converts feeling into meaning and creative expression.

## 7. WHAT MUST NOT BE USED AS A CRITERION

Do NOT infer SFP/SFJ from any of the following alone:
- consumer orientation;
- advertising;
- pleasure;
- lifestyle branding;
- community rhetoric;
- gamification;
- subscriptions;
- low wages;
- high turnover;
- MLM/referrals;
- emotional friendliness;
- information asymmetry;
- hierarchy;
- platform control;
- rewards;
- social media activity;
- influencer marketing;
- aspirational imagery;
- social proof;
- scripted friendliness;
- limited bargaining power.

Explicitly prohibit:
`consumer-facing + emotional branding + hierarchy + low wages = SFP/SFJ`

Required structure:
`sensory/emotional desire → subjectively voluntary engagement → internally controlled participation → repeated engagement → asymmetric retention of resource value`

## 8. DOCUMENTARY OBSERVABILITY

Documentary hypothesis support should show the full chain across multiple source types:
`desire / emotional engagement → voluntary participation → organised repetition → asymmetric resource retention`

Potential sources:
- marketing/engagement materials;
- contracts/participation terms;
- compensation plans;
- referral/recruitment systems;
- platform/product rules;
- retention metrics;
- access-rights/role matrices;
- internal manuals/scripts.

Potential evidence:
- desire/lifestyle/belonging/recognition/choice as entry/retention framing;
- formal participation boundaries;
- value-distribution mechanics;
- repeated-consumption/recruitment incentives;
- retention/reactivation architecture;
- concentrated control over compensation/data/clients/rankings/rules;
- standardised emotional interaction.

Strongest documentary pattern:
`desire-based attraction + repeated-participation architecture + concentrated control + evidence of disproportionate resource retention`

Low-observability:
- real subjective voluntariness;
- actual emotional engagement;
- real bargaining power;
- participant understanding;
- informal pressure/emotional sanctions;
- actual replaceability;
- real controller behind formal structure;
- true degree of surplus extraction.

Cautions:
- formal consent ≠ proof of experienced freedom;
- marketing intent ≠ proof of actual emotional engagement;
- nominal compensation ≠ proof of asymmetric extraction;
- formal rights ≠ proof of real bargaining power.

Documents usually show SFJ architecture better than lived SFP experience.

**`SFP/SFJ is an Environment where sensory and emotional desire is converted into voluntary repeated participation, while an internal structure organises that participation and retains the resulting resource flow.`**

Minimum chain:
`desire → voluntary repeated participation → internal organisation → retained resource flow`
