# Owner-Accepted Environment Card 5 — NF/SFP

**Status:** Owner-accepted semantics integrated in the CORR2 frozen set. Not independently verified or governance-bound by this act.

## 1. Whole-Environment description

`NF/SFP` is an expressive-aesthetic Environment centred on genuine creative self-expression, embodied meaning, and emotionally authentic connection. Its activity consumes resources and lacks an internal mechanism for replenishment or accountability. `[NF_SFP_REF, Overview!C6:C10,C33:C35,C46:C51]`

## 2. Core logic

NF meaning is expressed through immediate sensory-feeling activity. Creative or relational expression is treated as genuine when it carries lived meaning rather than merely repeating convention or serving formal hierarchy. `[NF_SFP_REF, Overview!C7:C10,C14:C17]`

## 3. Resource relation

The v1.7 formula is: **spending resources under a respectable-looking motive → lack of control in pleasures**. The foundation clarifies this as genuine expenditure into creative expression, aesthetic experience, and relational warmth without an internal replenishment or accountability mechanism. The motive may be genuinely good; the spending may still be uncontrolled. `[v1.7/03 §2, line 43] [NF_SFP_REF, Overview!C7,C13,C18,C33:C35,C46:C51]`

## 4. Power / control

The foundation describes authority through aesthetic credibility and decisions through emotional resonance rather than formal title. It also identifies the absence of an internal accountability mechanism for resource use. These are accepted semantic descriptions, not operational classification rules. `Power` is not a current canonical `NF/SFP` mechanism. `[OWNER-CORR2-NFSFP] [NF_SFP_REF, Overview!C14:C18]`

## 5. Phenomenon — outward appearance

The outward NF form presents creative expression, aesthetic experience, and relational activity as carriers of genuine human meaning. `[NF_SFP_REF, Overview!C7:C10]`

## 6. Essence — governing internal structure

The SFP inner structure is immediate sensory-feeling expression and authentic emotional engagement. Normal `NF/SFP` consists of genuine creative self-expression, authentic emotional engagement, aesthetic meaning, and human connection. Its resource-spending dynamic continues without an internal regeneration or accountability mechanism. `[OWNER-CORR2-NFSFP] [NF_SFP_REF, Overview!C7,C17:C18,C33:C35]`

## 7. Necessary features

The foundation treats authentic creative or emotional expression as necessary to this Environment and identifies continuous resource expenditure without internal replenishment as its structural constraint. No specific occupation, artistic identity, or psychological diagnosis is necessary. `[NF_SFP_REF, Overview!C7,C10,C33]`

## 8. Typical but not required features

Small creative communities, theatre, music, art, aesthetic practice, emotionally open conflict processing, and external patronage or institutional support are source examples or stabilising contexts, not exclusive markers. `[NF_SFP_REF, Overview!C9,C17,C24,C36]`

## 9. Nearest-Environment distinction

Unlike `SFJ/SFP`, meaning remains fluid and personally expressed rather than anchored mainly in tradition and preservation. Unlike `NF/SFJ`, values are not yet codified into doctrine and hierarchy. `[NF_SFP_REF, Overview!C7:C8,C27,C29,C51]`

## 10. Actor-level conditions

The ST-6 formation clause links the Environment to interaction between people with creative motivation and people oriented toward immediate physical pleasure. This remains actor→Environment context and is not reversed into a classifier. `[C-26A; СТ-6, ¶75; X6]`

## 11. Environment → actor effects

The degradation path is `NF/SFP → NF/SP`: as the NF meaning-anchor weakens, the SFP pleasure dimension may dominate through sensory stimulation, physical or sexual pleasure, unstable emotional connection, psychoactive stimulation, and destructive behavioural patterns. This is movement away from normal `NF/SFP`, not its hidden Essence. Direction remains Environment→actor and must not be reversed into assignment. `[OWNER-CORR2-NFSFP] [C-27; СТ-6, ¶76; X6] [NF_SFP_REF, Overview!C23,C28,C50]`

## 12. What must NOT be used as a criterion

Do not classify from creative work, artistry, emotional openness, declared values, pleasure, sexuality, one resource loss, or a drive for power alone. Do not treat sexuality, destructive patterns, or `power` as the hidden normal Essence. Do not treat psychological wording as documentary proof. Do not infer hidden motive from public language or turn the foundation's timelines into fixed prediction intervals. `[OWNER-CORR2-NFSFP]`
