# Owner-Accepted Environment Card 3 — NT/STP

**Status:** Owner-accepted semantics integrated in the CORR2 frozen set. Not independently verified or governance-bound by this act.

## 1. Whole-Environment description

`NT/STP` applies conceptual and analytical intelligence through immediate tactical or physical testing. Ideas must prove themselves empirically in the problem at hand. `[OWNER-NTSTP] [NT_STP_REF, Overview!C6:C10]`

## 2. Core logic

**Concept → immediate empirical test → keep, discard, or iterate → working result.** The Environment resolves uncertainty by running experiments and using the result to choose the next action. `[OWNER-NTSTP] [NT_STP_REF, Overview!C6:C8,C15:C17]`

## 3. Resource relation

**Risk appetite → resources staked on unproven solutions → empirical test → redistribution toward what works.** Failed solutions lose their stake; successful solutions attract or receive more resources. `[OWNER-NTSTP] [v1.7/03 §2, line 42] [NT_STP_REF, Overview!C36:C40]`

“Greedy” is non-moral shorthand for a resource-holder with enough risk appetite to stake existing resources on an unproven opportunity in pursuit of potential gain. No moral greed is implied. It does not require an outsized return, a crash-exit belief, fraud victimhood, or exploitation by an information-advantaged organiser. `[OWNER-NTSTP] [NT_STP_REF, Overview!C37]`

## 4. Power / control

Technical credibility and demonstrated ability matter. The empirical result is authoritative, not hierarchy, status, prior agreement, or the person who proposed the idea. “Absence of control mechanisms” means no external procedural, ethical or moral, authority-based, status-based, or prior-agreement arbiter governs redistribution. `[OWNER-CORR2-NTSTP] [NT_STP_REF, Overview!C14:C15,C36,C40]`

## 5. Phenomenon — outward appearance

The outward form is applied innovation, rapid prototyping, tactical technical work, and direct engagement with physical constraints. `[NT_STP_REF, Overview!C7:C10]`

## 6. Essence — governing internal structure

The internal rule is empirical selection: competing ideas or solutions are tested, failed ones are discarded or revised, and resources move toward the solutions that work. `[OWNER-NTSTP] [NT_STP_REF, Overview!C15:C17,C36:C40]`

## 7. Necessary features

Immediate empirical testing is necessary to the stated foundation logic. Continuous problem-solving is necessary to the foundation's innovation stance. No moral quality or fraud structure is necessary. `[NT_STP_REF, Overview!C15:C16]`

## 8. Typical but not required features

Rapid prototyping, engineering under physical constraints, field experiments, and close integration of conceptual and practical work are typical examples, not exclusive criteria. `[NT_STP_REF, Overview!C9,C20:C23]`

The ST-6 passages about financial pyramids, information asymmetry, organiser-controlled extraction, and collapse are `LEGACY / SUPERSEDED NT/STP SEMANTICS — PROVENANCE ONLY`. They do not define, illustrate, classify, or support inference about current `NT/STP`. `[OWNER-CORR2-NTSTP] [C-22:C-25; СТ-6, ¶69, ¶71–¶73]`

## 9. Nearest-Environment distinction

`NT/STJ`: strategy → structured implementation → durable or scalable system. `NT/STP`: concept → immediate physical or empirical test → iterate → result now. This is a bounded distinction, not a claim that either Environment never uses the other's methods. `[OWNER-NTSTP] [NT_STP_REF, Overview!C8]`

## 10. Actor-level conditions

Risk appetite is relevant only in the resource relation: willingness to stake existing resources on an unproven opportunity. It is not a moral trait or a standalone Environment classifier. `[OWNER-NTSTP] [NT_STP_REF, Overview!C37]`

## 11. Environment → actor effects

The foundation describes high tolerance for intelligent experimental failure and pressure toward rapid iteration. These remain candidate Environment effects and must not be reversed into assignment rules. `[NT_STP_REF, Overview!C21:C23]`

## 12. What must NOT be used as a criterion

Do not classify from moral greed, wealth, ambition, a crypto label, a financial loss, an exceptional-return promise, information asymmetry, fraud victimhood, organiser control, or later collapse. Do not treat one successful experiment or generic innovation language as sufficient. `[OWNER-NTSTP] [C-22:C-25]`
