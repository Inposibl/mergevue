# Owner-Accepted Environment Card 7 — SFJ/SFP

**Status:** Owner-accepted semantics integrated in the CORR3 refrozen set. Not independently verified or governance-bound by this act.

## 1. Whole-Environment description

`SFJ/SFP` is a stable, predictable Environment organised around belonging, tradition, physical comfort, and preservation of resources within trusted relationships. Its inner orientation is immediate emotional-sensory enjoyment and pleasure in interaction with others. `[C-34; СТ-7, ¶617] [C-33 RA-3B; СТ-7, ¶613; X3] [SFJ_SFP_REF, Overview!C6:C9]`

The Environment is the baseline Everyday/Moral interaction environment in which social continuity, belonging, trusted relationships and preservation of accumulated resources take precedence over innovation, competition or abstract knowledge production. `[OWNER-CORR2-SFJSFP]`

## 2. Core logic

The Environment preserves what has been accumulated through durable social ties. Tradition means social continuity reproduced through established relationships, roles, practices, and expectations across time. Loyalty means reciprocal preservation of in-group belonging. Decisions and status tend to follow loyalty, tenure, and care within the in-group rather than abstract performance alone. `[OWNER-CORR2-SFJSFP] [SFJ_SFP_REF, Overview!C12:C16,C19:C22,C37:C41]`

## 3. Resource relation

The v1.7 formula is: **preserving acquired resources → social ties**. The foundation clarifies that the relationship network is itself the principal conserved resource and that material comfort, belonging, status, and trust circulate mainly within it. `[v1.7/03 §2, line 38] [SFJ_SFP_REF, Overview!C37:C41]`

## 4. Power / control

Authority is informal and follows seniority, tenure, trust, and demonstrated loyalty. In-group membership may be ascribed through family, kinship, origin, or another durable pre-existing relation, or acquired through repeated loyalty, reliability, care, and commitment. Initial membership and trusted standing are distinct: deep trust, resource access, protection, and influence normally depend on demonstrated loyalty over time. Decisions seek relational safety and preservation of group harmony. `[OWNER-CORR2-SFJSFP] [SFJ_SFP_REF, Overview!C13:C15,C19:C22]`

## 5. Phenomenon — outward appearance

The SFJ outward form is tradition, loyalty, care, continuity, and visible belonging. Tradition is continuity through durable social relationships, not an isolated ritual or static custom. `[OWNER-CORR2-SFJSFP] [SFJ_SFP_REF, Overview!C7:C9]`

## 6. Essence — governing internal structure

The SFP inner drive is comfort, immediate emotional-sensory enjoyment, and pleasure in close interaction. `[C-33 RA-3B; X3] [SFJ_SFP_REF, Overview!C7:C8]`

## 7. Necessary features

The Environment requires preservation through an established relationship network. Belonging may be ascribed or acquired; deep trust requires loyalty demonstrated over time. No family structure, traditional industry, village setting, or specific ritual is necessary. `[OWNER-CORR2-SFJSFP] [SFJ_SFP_REF, Overview!C12:C22,C37:C41]`

## 8. Typical but not required features

Family and community settings, traditional production, comfort, security, long tenure, and gradual change introduced by trusted insiders are typical foundation examples or regularities. `[C-34] [SFJ_SFP_REF, Overview!C9,C12:C16,C22]`

## 9. Nearest-Environment distinction

Unlike `STJ/STP`, the SJ/SP base is expressed through the SF axis as care and belonging rather than through the ST axis as competition and dominance. Unlike `SFP/SFJ`, this Environment preserves existing resources and continuity through trusted relationships rather than converting desire into repeated participation and retained resource flow. `[OWNER-CORR2-SFJSFP] [OWNER-CORR2-SFPSFJ] [SFJ_SFP_REF, Overview!C7:C8]`

## 10. Actor-level conditions

Family-valued `C-33 RA-3A` context does not attribute an actor condition to `SFJ/SFP` alone and cannot be reversed into assignment. `[C-33 RA-3A; X6]`

## 11. Environment → actor effects

The source says the Environment develops sensitivity to the mood of close people, loyalty, and capacity for compromise. Loyalty means reciprocal preservation of in-group belonging. Direction must not be reversed. `[OWNER-CORR2-SFJSFP] [C-36; СТ-7, ¶620; X6]`

## 12. What must NOT be used as a criterion

Do not classify from a family label, traditional sector, comfort, pleasure, emotionality, loyalty, tenure, ascribed membership, or one warm interaction. Initial membership does not by itself prove deep trust. Do not use actor type as the Environment definition. `[OWNER-CORR2-SFJSFP]`
