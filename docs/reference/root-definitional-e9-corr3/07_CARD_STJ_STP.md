# Owner-Accepted Environment Card 6 — STJ/STP

**Status:** Owner-accepted semantics integrated in the CORR2 frozen set. Not independently verified or governance-bound by this act.

## 1. Whole-Environment description

`STJ/STP` presents strict rules, rigid hierarchy, and control. Its inner drive is immediate competition, dominance, destruction, or appropriation within a zero-sum struggle for resources. `[C-35; СТ-7, ¶619] [C-33 RA-3B; СТ-7, ¶613; X3] [STJ_STP_REF, Overview!C6:C9]`

## 2. Core logic

The Environment holds together through here-and-now competition for primacy. Position depends on the capacity to take, control, and defend resources under explicit hierarchy. `[C-33 RA-3B; X3] [STJ_STP_REF, Overview!C12:C15,C19:C23]`

## 3. Resource relation

Current neutral resource logic is: **coercive redistribution → resources move toward actors able to take, control, and defend them**. “Weak” means structurally unable to defend a resource claim because the actor lacks sufficient force, tactical cunning, or institutional position. It is a relational position in the current resource struggle, not a general judgment of personal weakness. Registry: **weak = undefended resource-holder in the current power relation**. `[OWNER-CORR2-STJSTP] [STJ_STP_REF, Overview!C39:C43]`

The v1.7 word “unfair” is a legacy evaluative description, not current structural semantics. A violated prior agreement or impersonal fairness rule is not required. `[OWNER-CORR2-STJSTP] [v1.7/03 §2, line 37]`

## 4. Power / control

Authority is explicit and hierarchical. It is enforced through demonstrated strength, punishment capacity, and control over resources. Decisions are fast and top-down; consultation may occur as information gathering but does not displace the decision-maker. `[C-35] [STJ_STP_REF, Overview!C13:C15]`

## 5. Phenomenon — outward appearance

The STJ outward form is strict rules, visible hierarchy, control, tribute, and punishment for defection. `[STJ_STP_REF, Overview!C7:C9]`

## 6. Essence — governing internal structure

The STP inner drive is competition, tactical dominance, destruction, or appropriation in the here-and-now struggle for primacy. `[C-33 RA-3B; X3] [STJ_STP_REF, Overview!C7:C8]`

## 7. Necessary features

The foundation requires an explicit dominance hierarchy and zero-sum resource struggle for this semantic form. No criminal setting, physical violence, or single observed hard rule is necessary. `[STJ_STP_REF, Overview!C7:C15]`

## 8. Typical but not required features

Criminal networks, punitive management, territorial control, tactical cunning, and visible enforcement are foundation examples or regularities, not exclusive markers. `[C-35] [C-36] [STJ_STP_REF, Overview!C9,C19:C23,C30]`

## 9. Nearest-Environment distinction

Unlike `SFJ/SFP`, the shared SJ/SP base is expressed through the ST axis as dominance rather than through the SF axis as care and belonging. Unlike `NT/STJ`, authority rests on direct dominance rather than demonstrable strategic or technological performance. `[STJ_STP_REF, Overview!C7:C8] [NT_STJ_REF, Overview!C10]`

## 10. Actor-level conditions

Family-valued `C-33 RA-3A` context does not attribute an actor condition to `STJ/STP` alone and cannot be reversed into assignment. `[C-33 RA-3A; X6]`

## 11. Environment → actor effects

The source says the Environment develops cunning, opponent anticipation, and ruthlessness toward structurally undefended resource-holders. “Weak” carries only the Owner-defined relational meaning above. Direction must not be reversed. `[OWNER-CORR2-STJSTP] [C-36; СТ-7, ¶620; X6]`

## 12. What must NOT be used as a criterion

Do not classify from one competitive act, one hard rule, a criminal example, the words “weak” or “unfair,” or an asserted fairness violation. Do not treat actor type, physical force, or a specific institution as necessary. `[OWNER-CORR2-STJSTP]`
