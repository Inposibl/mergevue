# Owner-Accepted Environment Card 4 — NF/SFJ

**Status:** Owner-accepted semantics integrated in the CORR2 frozen set. Not independently verified or governance-bound by this act.

## 1. Whole-Environment description

`NF/SFJ` presents a belief system as absolute truth. Its internal structure concentrates material and psychological resources upward in the group that controls interpretation. It uses ideology and central control to preserve that order. `[OWNER-CORR2-NFSFJ] [C-18; СТ-6, ¶63] [C-32; СТ-7, ¶610] [NF_SFJ_REF, Overview!C7,C14,C32:C36]`

## 2. Core logic

The Environment turns stated values into a stable hierarchy. Rules bind lower levels differently from the controlling group. Support follows alignment with the accepted belief system. `[C-18; СТ-6, ¶63] [C-19; СТ-6, ¶64] [NF_SFJ_REF, Overview!C7,C10,C14:C15]`

## 3. Resource relation

Resource concentration in the controlling group is a defining internal mechanism: **sacred or absolute belief system → control over interpretation → hierarchical compliance → upward resource transfer**. Compactly: **control of meaning → control of people → upward resource extraction**. Resources include money, time, energy, attention, autonomy, and other usable material or psychological resources. `[OWNER-CORR2-NFSFJ] [NF_SFJ_REF, Overview!C32:C36]`

The v1.7 formula **sacred war for resources → people's faith** remains source context for this Owner-defined mechanism. The `C-39` comparison about wealth and comfortable life remains pairwise context rather than the defining resource relation. `[v1.7/03 §2, line 41] [C-39; СТ-7, ¶626]`

## 4. Power / control

A controlling group holds the upper position. Lower levels are subject to limits from which upper levels are exempt. Control over interpretation supports hierarchical compliance and upward resource transfer. `[OWNER-CORR2-NFSFJ] [C-18; СТ-6, ¶63]`

The sacrificial Other is a defining persistence mechanism, not the primary resource logic or complete definition. An external enemy or threat may be amplified for consolidation. When external opposition is insufficient, internal dissenters, heretics, or supposed enemy agents are designated. Its function is **belief consolidation → group cohesion → preservation of elite position**. Registry: **NF/SFJ persistence = sacred unity requires a sacrificial Other**. `[OWNER-CORR2-NFSFJ] [C-19; СТ-6, ¶64] [NF_SFJ_REF, Overview!C10,C17,C22]`

## 5. Phenomenon — outward appearance

A belief system is presented to participants as absolute truth. `[C-18; СТ-6, ¶63; X4]`

`ANALYST FORMALIZATION`: Within `NF/SFJ`, this is the source-faithful structural meaning used instead of the evaluative word “dogmatic” (`«догматической»`). `[C-18; X2]`

## 6. Essence — governing internal structure

Resources are redistributed toward the group that controls the system. `[C-18; СТ-6, ¶63; X4]`

## 7. Necessary features

Current Owner authority establishes two defining mechanisms: upward concentration of material and psychological resources through control of meaning, and reproduction of a sacrificial Other to preserve sacred unity and elite position. Neither is converted here into a single-document or operational classification rule. `[OWNER-CORR2-NFSFJ]`

## 8. Typical but not required features

- Religious, sectarian, imperial, and monarchic systems are source examples, not required forms. `[C-18; СТ-6, ¶63] [C-32; СТ-7, ¶610]`
- The source describes a formation path from `SFJ/SFP`, where pleasure becomes culture, ritual, tradition, and then ideology. This is context, not an automatic or reversible transition rule. `[C-37; СТ-7, ¶622]`
- Long duration and repeated exclusion of named internal categories are source regularities, not necessary conditions. `[C-19; СТ-6, ¶64]`

## 9. Nearest-Environment distinction

Unlike `NF/NT`, `NF/SFJ` presents a settled belief as absolute truth and protects a controlling hierarchy rather than keeping knowledge open to argument. Unlike normal `NF/SFP`, its governing mechanism concentrates resources through ideology and hierarchy rather than sustaining fluid authentic creative expression and human connection. `[OWNER-CORR2-NFSFJ] [OWNER-CORR2-NFSFP] [C-11; СТ-6, ¶50] [C-18; СТ-6, ¶63]`

## 10. Actor-level conditions

No Environment-specific actor→Environment condition is lawfully established for this card.

## 11. Environment → actor effects

Degradation means progressive narrowing and suppression of autonomous reflective capacity. For ordinary participants this includes growing dependence on the accepted belief framework and reduced capacity to test claims independently, hold alternative explanations, revise beliefs, decide autonomously, or create outside permitted doctrine. For the controlling elite it additionally includes weakening of reality-testing as the system rewards management of collective belief rather than competence in understanding reality. This is an Environment→actor effect and must not be reversed into a classifier. It is not a literal necessary decline in formal developmental stage or consciousness level. `[OWNER-CORR2-NFSFJ] [C-20; СТ-6, ¶65; X6]`

## 12. What must NOT be used as a criterion

Do not classify from ideology, religion, empire, hierarchy, “dogmatic,” a claimed enemy, or one observed upward transfer alone. Do not use the absence of an external threat as an exclusion rule; documents cannot certify that absence. Do not infer a literal developmental-stage decline from degradation. Do not use the expository label “manipulative” as an automatic criterion. `[OWNER-CORR2-NFSFJ] [C-18] [C-19] [C-20] [C-21; X5]`
