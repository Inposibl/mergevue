## 1. Correction of §5 — Required P-S scientific-integrity assessment

**Replace parent §5, including SI-1–SI-6, with the following:**

### 5.1 General acceptance rule

Removing blanket individual P-S causal-category disposition from the CORR4 §M prerequisite is scientifically permissible only if an independently verified replacement assessment establishes that the eligibility process has been evaluated for the scientific risks which the original prerequisite could have exposed, including:

- Incorrect exclusion of analytically relevant facts.
- Shared A/B eligibility errors not visible in their disagreement count.
- Unresolved definition, mechanism-registry or grammar deficiencies material to downstream coding.
- Distortion of the D-domain and organizational-side composition of the selected pilot.
- Material effects on the interpretation or admissibility of downstream analytical conclusions.

A correct R-01 measurement alone is never sufficient to satisfy this assessment.

The amendment does not assume that reviewing disagreement categories and independently assessing eligibility validity are identical scientific operations.

### SI-0 — Mandatory pre-registered assessment protocol

Before any historical scientific-integrity assessment may be treated as decisive, an exact assessment protocol must be:

1. Authored as a separate, version-identified methodological artifact.
2. Independently verified by a non-author.
3. Explicitly accepted by Owner.
4. Fixed before the assessment's outcome-relevant observations are inspected or used to tune the procedure.
5. Bound to a reproducible identity and applicable frozen scientific inputs.

The protocol must identify its eligible evaluators, permissible information, independence conditions, sampling or census rule, scientific reference standard, adjudication method, evaluation endpoints, falsifiers and stopping criteria.

An assessment protocol produced after reviewing outcome-relevant evidence may not claim prospective pre-registration. If such evidence has already been exposed, the limitation must be recorded, and the Owner must separately determine an admissible blinded or otherwise defensible evaluation design.

**Fail-closed rule:** If the protocol is absent, incomplete, independently unverified, not Owner-accepted or not fixed at the required boundary, SI-6 cannot yield `SCIENTIFICALLY_SUFFICIENT_WITHIN_BOUND`.

### SI-1 — Frozen measurement and input integrity

The assessment must preserve and verify:

- The 905 facts and 13 D-domain question identities.
- The two frozen 11,765-mark A/B records.
- The exact 1,094 P-S disagreement ledger.
- The deterministic A∩B eligibility rule.
- The original sample-selection algorithm and resulting frozen selection.
- The outcome-hidden information boundary.

This measurement records inter-stratifier agreement. It does not validate any individual eligibility judgment.

No alternative marks or modified sample may replace the frozen historical records.

### SI-2 — Mandatory examination strata

The assessment protocol must explicitly cover the following four disjoint A/B mark strata:

- `YES/YES`
- `YES/NO`
- `NO/YES`
- `NO/NO`

The protocol must evaluate the consequences of these strata across:

- The 13 frozen D domains.
- Organizational sides represented in the frozen corpus.
- The originally selected and excluded fact populations, where these distinctions are relevant to the claim under test.

The protocol must state how its examination units are selected, how empty or small cells are treated, and which claims are valid for each evaluated layer.

Particular scrutiny is required for jointly negative `NO/NO` judgments because agreement alone cannot reveal a common false exclusion.

The accepted historical measurement counts, including the observed D-domain asymmetries, may be used to define the examination frame. They must not be used retrospectively to tune a purportedly prospective error threshold.

#### Coverage rule

For each required stratum/domain/side combination, the assessment record must provide one of:

- `EVALUATED`: an admissible independently reproduced result under the fixed protocol.
- `STRUCTURALLY_EMPTY`: the combination has no member, established from the frozen census.
- `NOT_ESTABLISHED`: there is no sufficient examination result for that combination.

A merely unsampled nonempty combination must never be described as structurally empty.

The protocol must predefine whether complete examination, bounded sampling, or another scientifically justified design is used.

No particular numerical sample size is established by this amendment.

#### Claim ceiling

An `EVALUATED` combination establishes only the claim that the protocol and evidence actually support.

Coverage of some combinations never implies validity of the unexamined population.

A scientifically material `NOT_ESTABLISHED` combination prevents an unconditional sufficiency determination.

### SI-3 — Independent eligibility reference and missed-relevance detection

The protocol must define an independent way to evaluate whether a fact is relevant to a given neutral D-domain question for the intended downstream use.

That reference must:

1. Apply the fixed neutral observational meaning of the D question.
2. Avoid using A/B agreement as its own correctness oracle.
3. Avoid using downstream M, TT, Environment or transaction outcomes as retroactive justifications for the original eligibility judgment.
4. Preserve evaluator independence and appropriate blindness.
5. Document the primary factual basis and its scope for each evaluated judgment.
6. Permit `NOT_DETERMINABLE` when the permitted evidence cannot establish relevance.

The protocol must be capable of identifying at least the following distinct cases:

- A fact accepted by A∩B that the independent reference finds irrelevant.
- A fact excluded by A∩B that the independent reference finds relevant.
- A fact on which the reference is unable to decide.
- Evidence suggesting a neutral-domain definition ambiguity.
- Evidence suggesting a missing mechanism-registry concept or grammar deficiency requiring separate scientific/schema review.

A potential `MISSING_M` or `GRAMMAR_GAP` finding is a referral candidate, not an automatic change to the frozen registry or an automatically accepted §L-4 category assignment.

If no scientifically admissible reference can distinguish excluded relevant facts from genuinely irrelevant facts under the preserved information boundaries, eligibility validity is `NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

The protocol may not substitute reviewer consensus, A/B majority, R-01 or evidence silence for an independent correctness reference.

### SI-4 — A∩B consequences and downstream materiality

The assessment must define how identified or credibly bounded eligibility errors affect the intended downstream use.

At minimum, the protocol must address:

1. False inclusion and false exclusion relative to the independent reference.
2. Whether error patterns are concentrated in particular D domains or organizational sides.
3. Whether excluded relevant facts could change the original selected pilot's domain/side composition or an applicable coverage determination.
4. Whether identified omissions expose a needed definition, grammar or registry correction.
5. Whether the consequences threaten validity of the post-pilot analytical contract.

Any counterfactual evaluation of changed eligibility or selection must be separately identified as diagnostic. It must never overwrite the frozen historical A/B marks or pilot sample.

The protocol must specify a reproducible materiality decision rule before applying it to assessment results.

That rule must identify:

- The scientific endpoint being protected.
- The evidence needed to determine an effect.
- What counts as a material failure for that endpoint.
- How uncertain or unobserved effects are represented.
- Which findings require escalation for versioned correction, independent re-verification or a new Owner decision.

No arbitrary numeric error allowance, agreement cutoff, confidence threshold or materially significant effect size is imposed by this candidate.

If a numerical or categorical threshold is necessary, its basis and exact value must be justified in the separate protocol and independently reviewed before Owner acceptance.

The auditor or scientific executor may not choose a convenient threshold after observing the results.

### SI-5 — Mandatory falsifiers and stop conditions

The protocol must contain executable or independently reviewable detection criteria for at least these failure modes:

**F-PS-01 — Shared false exclusion:** Independently established relevant facts occur in the A/B `NO/NO` stratum, with a consequence exceeding the accepted materiality rule.

**F-PS-02 — Directional eligibility distortion:** An independently established error pattern in `YES/NO` or `NO/YES` has a material adverse consequence for the intended downstream use.

**F-PS-03 — Unexamined material layer:** A required nonempty D-domain, organizational-side or mark-stratum layer lacks enough admissible evidence to support the intended sufficiency claim.

**F-PS-04 — Invalid reference:** The claimed independent eligibility reference is circular, outcome-contaminated, methodologically inadmissible or insufficiently reproducible.

**F-PS-05 — Contract-definition defect:** The assessment identifies a relevant definition, mechanism-registry or grammar deficiency that triggers an applicable unresolved scientific correction obligation.

**F-PS-06 — Downstream invalidity:** An independently demonstrated eligibility defect materially changes or invalidates an applicable sampling, coverage or analytical-validity determination.

**F-PS-07 — Uncontrolled protocol change:** Assessment endpoints, decision thresholds, sample rules or eligibility-reference rules are retrospectively altered without an independently reviewed and Owner-authorized version transition.

The assessment must distinguish:

- `FIRED`: admissible evidence establishes the specified failure condition.
- `SURVIVED_WITHIN_TESTED_BOUND`: a stated falsifier was tested and did not fire within an explicitly declared evidentiary bound.
- `NOT_TESTED`: the required evidence or procedure is absent.
- `NOT_DETERMINABLE`: the permitted evidence cannot resolve the specified condition.

`SURVIVED_WITHIN_TESTED_BOUND` never means universal absence of bias.

Where a required falsifier is `FIRED`, scientific sufficiency is blocked pending the applicable correction or separate Owner decision.

Where a required falsifier is `NOT_TESTED` or `NOT_DETERMINABLE`, an unconditional sufficiency result is prohibited.

No new fact classification is authorized by defining these failure conditions.

### SI-6 — Scientific sufficiency decision

The permitted final assessment outcomes remain:

- `SCIENTIFICALLY_SUFFICIENT_WITHIN_BOUND`
- `SCIENTIFICALLY_INSUFFICIENT`
- `NOT_ESTABLISHED`

#### Preconditions for `SCIENTIFICALLY_SUFFICIENT_WITHIN_BOUND`

Every condition below must be satisfied:

1. SI-0 protocol approval and identity requirements are met.
2. SI-1 measurement/input integrity is independently established.
3. SI-2 examination-frame coverage is established to the extent required by the pre-accepted scientific claim and assessment design.
4. SI-3 supplies an admissible independent eligibility reference for all conclusions that require such a reference.
5. SI-4 materiality has been evaluated under a previously accepted reproducible rule.
6. SI-5 required falsifiers have been tested, with no unresolved material failure or unaddressed mandatory evidentiary gap.
7. All detected issues requiring schema review, affected-cell correction or independent re-verification have been separately tracked and remain blocking until lawfully disposed.
8. The independent verifier confirms that the assessment supports its stated downstream-use claim without generalizing beyond the tested evidence.
9. Owner has expressly accepted any methodological policy or threshold decisions required to interpret the scientific result.

A procedurally complete report lacking necessary scientific evidence does not meet these preconditions.

#### `SCIENTIFICALLY_INSUFFICIENT`

Use this state when admissible evidence establishes a material violation of the accepted scientific-validity criteria.

The report must identify the failed criterion, affected scope and required correction or Owner decision.

#### `NOT_ESTABLISHED`

Use this state whenever a mandatory rule, pre-approved threshold, admissible reference, required examination layer, necessary falsifier or materiality conclusion is unavailable or unresolved.

Unknown must not be reported as success.

#### Decision authority

An independent scientific assessment may recommend sufficiency only within its explicitly tested bounds.

It cannot authorize the removal of a controlling methodological obligation on its own.

The Owner remains the authority for acceptance of the amended methodology and any separate post-pilot contract decision.
