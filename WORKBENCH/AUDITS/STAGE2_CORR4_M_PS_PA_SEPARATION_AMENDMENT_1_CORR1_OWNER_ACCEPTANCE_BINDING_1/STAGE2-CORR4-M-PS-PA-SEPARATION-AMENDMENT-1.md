## 1. Purpose and change boundary

This candidate proposes a bounded amendment to the disagreement-classification prerequisite in CORR4 §M.

The purpose is to distinguish:

1. Measurement of stratifier agreement over frozen P-S records.
2. Scientific review of stratifier eligibility validity and possible systematic distortions.
3. Scientific disposition of analytical-coder disagreements in P-A.

The amendment removes mandatory individual causal-category disposition of every P-S tuple **as an automatic §M prerequisite**, subject to independent validation of the replacement scientific-integrity gates defined below.

It does not declare those gates satisfied.

No scientific classification, evidence alteration, contract freeze or downstream execution is performed by this candidate.

## 2. Frozen populations

## 3. Proposed replacement interpretation of CORR4 §L-2

Replace the unrestricted linkage between stratification disagreement measurement and individual §L-4 classification with:

> Stratification agreement is measured and reported over the complete frozen per-(fact, D) A/B mark population. Disagreements are retained as individually traceable, immutable measurement records. For the bounded purpose of reporting R-01, individual assignment of CORR4 §L-4 causal categories to every P-S disagreement is not required.
>
> This exception does not establish stratifier correctness or eligibility validity. It does not modify the intersection eligibility rule, the predeclared sampling algorithm, the frozen pilot, or the requirement to assess scientific limitations that could materially affect the interpretation or admissibility of the downstream analytical results.
>
> If an independent scientific-validity investigation identifies a specific P-S tuple or subgroup that requires causal adjudication for an independently established scientific reason, the required scope and evidence threshold must be expressly authorized. Such targeted investigation must not silently expand into automatic classification of the entire P-S population.

The existing L-2 outcome-hidden, blinding, D-domain, canonical-hash and intersection rules remain unchanged.

## 5. Required P-S scientific-integrity assessment

Removing blanket individual P-S classification from §M must not be treated as removing scrutiny of the eligibility process.

Before the post-pilot contract can be presented for Owner acceptance, a separately authorized, independent scientific assessment must determine whether the frozen stratification process provides sufficient evidentiary support for its intended downstream use.

At minimum, the assessment must address:

### SI-1 — Measurement integrity

Confirm the identities of the frozen A/B marks, neutral D questions, population denominator, disagreement ledger and deterministic R-01 computation.

Confirm that no outcome, Environment, M-registry or analytical-coder information was unlawfully introduced into the original stratification input.

An independently verified historical process-evidence sufficiency determination may be carried forward within its accepted scope; no new A/B stratification is authorized or required by this clause.

### SI-2 — Asymmetric eligibility behavior

Examine differences between A and B in positive eligibility rates and disagreement directions, including D-domain-level asymmetries.

An asymmetry is a diagnostic signal, not independent proof of coder error or bias.

The assessment must explicitly distinguish observed directional differences from established systematic eligibility distortion.

### SI-3 — Intersection-rule consequences

Evaluate whether the accepted A∩B eligibility rule creates a material, identifiable risk of excluding analytically relevant facts or entire D-domain/organizational-side classes.

Any claim of absence of distortion requires an appropriate independent evidentiary basis. A high R-01 score alone is insufficient.

No automatic change to A∩B is authorized.

### SI-4 — Sampling and coverage integrity

Determine whether the frozen pilot was selected according to the predeclared selection and exclusion rules.

Preserve EMPTY_STRATUM, ONE_SIDE_ONLY, NON_ANALYTICAL shortfall and other recorded limitations, as applicable.

Separately preserve the CORR4 §L-2 post-coding coverage obligations:

- `evidenceForm = DE-4`
- `planCurrentState = PLAN`
- `planCurrentState = CONDITIONAL`
- `planCurrentState = ELIGIBILITY_ONLY`

Each must be reported as `EXERCISED` or `COVERAGE_PATH_GAP` when the relevant post-coding evidence exists.

A coverage gap must not trigger unauthorized sample expansion, imputation, proxy construction, or retrospective Stage-1 metadata mapping.

### SI-5 — Scientific adequacy and falsification

Evaluate whether the proposed removal of blanket P-S classification leaves an independently mandatory scientific question unanswered.

The assessment must identify concrete potential falsifiers and failure modes rather than treating R-01 reproducibility as a sufficient validity test.

Where available evidence cannot resolve a potentially material concern, record `NOT_ESTABLISHED` and present the issue for a separate Owner decision.

### SI-6 — Decision sufficiency

The independent assessment must determine whether the accepted measurement, the preserved protocol, and any separately established scientific checks collectively provide sufficient support for removing blanket P-S classification as a §M prerequisite.

Permitted outcomes:

- `SCIENTIFICALLY_SUFFICIENT_WITHIN_BOUND`
- `SCIENTIFICALLY_INSUFFICIENT`
- `NOT_ESTABLISHED`

No threshold, statistical test, sample expansion or adjudication procedure is authorized merely by naming these outcomes. Their technical basis must be proposed, justified and independently reviewed before use.

A scientifically insufficient or unestablished outcome blocks post-pilot acceptance under the proposed amended route unless the Owner separately resolves the deficiency through an independently reviewable methodology decision.
