### D.2 General evidence rules [B]

- **G1 — Silence.** Absent or incomplete evidence gives `NOT_ESTABLISHED`, with falsifier `NOT_TESTED`. Silence never establishes that an event, link, application or world failed to occur. CORR4 §J-3 and §J-6 apply by analogy.
- **G2 — What fires.** A falsifier fires in only two ways:
  - **`CONTRARY_CONTENT`:** an authenticated record whose content, within the claim's declared scope, is logically incompatible with the claim.
  - **`EVIDENCE_DEFEAT`:** defeat of the authenticity, identity or a stated assumption of the very record the claim relied on. This sets the claim to `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. It does **not** establish the negation; the negation is a separate claim that needs its own `CONTRARY_CONTENT` record.
- **G3 — Alternative-record rule (replaces C104).** A record of another reading, producer, operation, copy, path or tuple counts as contrary only if all three hold:
  - **(a)** it is authenticated;
  - **(b)** it addresses the same scoped object: same actor, tuple, operation identity or covered window, and target;
  - **(c)** its content is incompatible with the claim, by explicit negation, explicit exclusivity, or a demonstrated structural exclusion.

  A stored value has exactly one terminal write. An affirmatively complete chain that shows that write's inputs excludes, from that value, any output it does not carry. That is a structural exclusion. A record that fails (a)–(c) is a `COMPATIBLE_ADDITIONAL_EVENT`. It is recorded, and the claim's status does not change. If it was the candidate tested against the claim, the claim's falsifier result is `SURVIVED`.
- **G4 — Where exclusivity comes from.** The record's content states it, or an authenticated definition of the record type makes the field single-valued and complete for that operation. A reviewer's assumption that an operation used only one reading is not exclusivity.
- **G5 — How far a falsifier travels.** A fired falsifier attaches to the claim whose declared content it contradicts. It reaches a compound claim only when the compound's declared content includes the contradicted proposition; for example, a DA claim that names R2 as B's operative reading. It never reaches a claim with a different scope: another operation, time, tuple, actor or target.
- **G6 — Thresholds.** Thresholds are not lowered. A hypothesis may be `SUPPORTED_NOT_CAUSAL` only if its exclusions were actually tested. Reviewer agreement supplies no record. `governanceDisposition` records an Owner ceiling, such as OD-09 D2, and never writes `ESTABLISHED` [E for the ceiling; B for the field].
- **G7 — "Off" in historical records.** A contrast mechanism counts as OFF in a historical world only through a competent affirmative record of its absence at matched scope and time (CORR4 §J-2 by analogy). Silence leaves that contrast slot unaddressed.

**Rules:**

- **Q1.** The quantifier, contrast set and background domain are declared before evaluation. The positive and the falsifier are evaluated against that same declaration.
- **Q2.** An existential witness never establishes a universal claim. A universal claim requires a finite, enumerated domain in which every member is represented.
- **Q3.** A world outside the domain is recorded in `outOfScopeObservations[]`. It never fires the claim.
- **Q4.** `STANDALONE_SUFFICIENCY` requires every other named mechanism of the fixture or trace to be fixed OFF in the contrast set. Moving a named mechanism into the background, for instance at ON, does not produce a standalone-sufficiency claim. Such a world is recorded only as an observed row.
- **Q5.** A historical world can witness SUFF@h_actual only when all three hold:
  - M is on;
  - every contrast mechanism is affirmatively recorded OFF (G7);
  - h_actual is declared by its evidenced variables.

  Actual production alone establishes no form of sufficiency [X]. Even when the observation meets the stated extensional `SUFF@h_actual` field, it establishes no disagreement causation without §F.2 target-specific difference evidence [B; Q8].
- **Q6.** No sufficiency form implies necessity, and no necessity form implies sufficiency. `SOLE_SUFFICIENT` stays unlawful [X].
- **Q7.** This section adopts no general doctrine of causal sufficiency. It fixes only what this rubric's fields mean [B]. CORR4 defines no complete sufficiency rule.
- **Q8 (T1; strict non-promotion).** The **canonical claim kind** for the three legacy `SUFF∃`, `SUFF@h0`, `SUFF∀` labels is `EXTENSIONAL_OUTCOME_CONDITION` (with quantified variants `EXISTS`, `FIXED`, `FORALL`). The legacy SUFF names remain only as cross-version fixture aliases. These fields describe *extensional outcome conditions in evidenced/fixture worlds*, **not** actual-cause theorems. In particular, an observed M-on / D-present world, even with other *named* mechanisms affirmed OFF, cannot show that the event **made the disagreement occur**. The conclusion `HISTORICAL_CONTRIBUTION_TO_DISAGREEMENT=ESTABLISHED` requires independent target-specific effect evidence under §F.2. No implication `SUFF → causal contribution → CLC` is permitted [X].
- **Q10 (G-IV1-MIN-02; complete conjunct falsification).** A fixed-background dependence claim is the conjunction `T(on,h0)=1 ∧ T(off,h0)=0`. An authenticated admissible `T(on,h0)=0` fires the **on conjunct** even if the off-world is missing. An authenticated admissible `T(off,h0)=1` fires the **off conjunct** even if the on-world is missing. When neither contradiction exists but at least one required world is absent, the whole claim is `NOT_ESTABLISHED / NOT_TESTED`. Firing one conjunct does not refute occurrence, production, or the other separately scoped claim. A different background h1 is not a falsifier. See R33a–d. This is a falsifier completeness correction, not a newly adopted theory of actual causation.
- **Q9 (T1; underdetermined alternatives).** The failure of a but-for test in one background refutes only the specific *difference-making* assertion for that background. It does **not** establish that an authenticated mechanism never contributed through an overdetermined or redundant path. Such broader historical-causation claims remain `NOT_ESTABLISHED` within this bounded rubric pending a separately authorized causal standard; their narrower authenticated event/path claims remain intact [B/P].
- **Conflicting records at the same h0.** If authenticated records of the same declared world disagree about T, SUFF@h0 is `FIRED` as declared. A narrower re-declaration that adds a distinguishing variable is a **new** claim. It is ESTABLISHED only if that variable is itself evidenced in both worlds. The fired record stays on file.

## E. Reading, Link R / Link S and DA protocol

### E.1 Admissibility and determinacy (preserved)

CORR4 §L-2 is unchanged [E]. No any-clause, dominant-clause, whole-question or new eligibility rule is added [X].

A reading is **materially admissible** when all three hold [B]:

- a competent reader can state it from the frozen question and the sealed fact before seeing the marks;
- it assigns YES or NO;
- no frozen exclusion removes it.

An **interpretation bound** is the set of readings the reviewer brings under one completeness statement. That statement lists:

- the readings considered;
- the feature of the question and fact that generates them;
- an exclusion for every class of further reading that could flip the output.

A class left unwritten is not an exclusion.

Four states:

1. Admissible readings give different outputs. There is no normative winner, and a single lawful output is NOT_ESTABLISHED.
2. Admissible readings give the same output and the bound is justified as complete. Determinacy is ESTABLISHED.
3. A defensible flip reading has not been excluded. Determinacy is NOT_ESTABLISHED.
4. Determinacy holds and the mark contradicts the shared output. This is a normative departure, not a historical cause.

Further rules:

- A unique wording is not required.
- Two agreeing readings are not a complete bound.
- Established determinacy and established output divergence cannot both hold on the same question text. That does not prove that every pair of category claims is impossible [X].

### E.2 Links

- **Link R:** a named reading was applied and was operative in producing the surviving mark.
- **Link S:** a named upstream event influenced selection or application of that reading.

These are separate claims, each with its own threshold and falsifier (§D.4). Missing Link S does not fire Link R, and missing Link R does not fire Link S.

### E.3 Event vs operative survival (Δ, MAJ-02)

`APPLIED_READING` is an event claim. `LINK_R` is a survival claim about one particular surviving mark.

- An actor may have several established application events: R1 at t1 and R2 at t2 (S20-A).
- An application whose Link R to the surviving mark is FIRED by an affirmative bypass stays an ESTABLISHED event (S20-C). It cannot supply DA condition 3.
- A record that "the operative reading for the surviving mark was R1" addresses Link R for R2. It does not address an earlier R2 application event (G5).
- If more than one reading has an established Link R on the same side and they give different outputs, that side is `OVERDETERMINATION_OR_UNRESOLVED`, unless the record identifies their roles.

### E.4 DA-witnessed positive threshold [B] (all required)

1. At least two materially admissible readings, no frozen tie-break, and no established shared output.
2. `APPLIED_READING` is ESTABLISHED, and not FIRED under §D.4, for A applying R1 and for B applying R2 to this fact.
3. `LINK_R` is ESTABLISHED, and not FIRED, on each side. Link S is not an input to this condition.
4. Neither stored mark is under `DEFEATING_ALTERNATIVE_PATH` or `OVERDETERMINATION_OR_UNRESOLVED`. Observed exposure with Link S NOT_ESTABLISHED leaves the grade at `READING_ONLY_OPERATIVE` and does not block.
5. The contrast holds the fact fixed and uses the two evidenced applications. No cross-reading swap [X]. No recomputing each side under its own reading and calling the match a null cause [X].
6. Only links whose own affirmative evidence is ESTABLISHED are used.

If 1–6 hold, the interpretation-divergence claim is ESTABLISHED at the DISAGREEMENT rung. Its existing category name is `DEFINITION_AMBIGUITY` [B]. No normative winner is selected, and sufficiency of either reading is not implied.

- A separate record: a question that names a frozen M or grammar surface, plus the observation that raises it.
- It may consult the registry and the grammar.
- It does not re-grade Lane A, and Lane A need not fail first.
- Schema insufficiency does not, by itself, cause an original eligibility disagreement. `causeLink = UNASSIGNED_PENDING_J1`. **J-1 remains OPEN** [P].
- **Sequencing.** A reviewer who will do Lane A Phase 1 on a tuple must seal before receiving that tuple's Lane B material. Receiving it before the seal is contamination (S28).

### G.4 Blind Phase 1 (P line 432, restored)

1. The custodian assembles the packet: tuple identity, sealed fact, neutral question, and nothing else. The custodian records `packetSha`, `issuedTo` and `issuedAt`.
2. The reviewer writes the interpretation bound, the rationale and the normative states, then seals. Recorded: `sealedBytesSha`, reviewer identity, `sealedAt`.
3. The custodian records `sealReceivedAt` before releasing anything further.
4. The sealed bytes are never edited. A reviewer's general prior familiarity with CORR4 is disclosed in `reviewerIdentity.priorExposure`. Tuple-specific forbidden information before the seal is contamination.

### G.5 Phase 2

1. The custodian reveals the marks and retrospective records `{id, sha}`. Recorded: `revealedAt`. If `revealedAt` precedes `sealedAt`, that is contamination.
2. The delta is appended, including "no change". The record is append-only.
3. Historical-mechanism claims are evaluated against authenticated records under §D.

### G.6 Contamination recovery (P lines 434–441, restored)

If the Phase-1 reviewer saw an original mark, the direction, an outcome, a prior rationale, Lane B material for the tuple, or any other Phase-1-forbidden information before the seal:

1. Record what was seen, when, and by whom.
2. Mark the Phase 1 `CONTAMINATED`. It cannot serve as blind proof.
3. Keep the contaminated record, the incident and the seal hash.
4. Do not edit it into a clean Phase 1.
5. A **different** reviewer who never received the forbidden information performs a new Phase 1. The same exposed reviewer repeating the pass does not restore blindness.
6. The reveal and later deltas stay append-only, and the contaminated pass stays in the chain.

### G.7 Independent reproduction and adjudication [B]

- Each claim type is reproduced at **its own** positive threshold by a non-author. `ADJUDICATED` attaches to that named claim only.
- **Normative claims** (determinacy, conclusion, reconciliation). The reproducer does their own Phase 1 on the same packet and seals **before** seeing the first reviewer's rationale or seal content, or any Phase-2 material: `independentReview.ownPhase1SealedBeforeRationale = TRUE`. Otherwise the reproduction is not blind and cannot adjudicate the normative claim (S26).
- **Historical-record claims** (event, linkage, production, application, Link R, Link S, contrasts). These are re-derived from the authenticated records (identity, SHA, locator), not from the first reviewer's summary: `recordsRederived = TRUE`. Blindness is not required.
- **Agreement and disagreement.** Reviewer agreement is not evidence of a historical mechanism (S15). Reviewer disagreement on a normative bound is a failure to reproduce: the claim stays not-ADJUDICATED. It is not a category.

### G.8 Ceiling

No real tuple is reviewed in this act. Current-review blindness says nothing about historical stratifiers beyond what authenticated records contain. Current hashes do not rule out universal historical contamination, mutation-and-revert, or unlogged exposure (OD-09 D2; IV1-ADV-02). Marks, ledger and sample stay byte-frozen [E: OD-09 §2.6].

## H. MISSING_M / GRAMMAR_GAP evidentiary boundaries

### H.1 Coverage vs instantiation (preserved)

**§H-2.** A proposition is selected by `preciseMeaning`. If none fits and a Tree consumer exists, the result is `UNMAPPED_OBSERVATION`. A coder never creates an M [E].

**§H-3.** Absent components go to `missingComponents`. They are never inferred. One missing component never proves MISSING_M [E].

**Coverage** works by subsumption under `preciseMeaning`, modality and scope, outside `explicitNonMeaning`. A narrower instance is covered without literal string equality. A stronger claim that the row withholds is not covered just by adding detail [B].

**Instantiation** is a separate record. A plan or authorization does not instantiate an occurrence modality.

**Frozen row** [E: CORR4 line 837]: `M-RES-EXPRESSIVE-EXPENDITURE` — "Resources are expended into creative, aesthetic or relational activity"; `preciseMeaning`: resource target = expressive activity; `explicitNonMeaning`: not that expression is authentic, not uncontrolled; components: c1 resources, c2 destination ∈ {creative, aesthetic, relational} activity.

- **Confirmed payment for an artistic workshop:** covered; c1 and c2 established; MISSING_M false (S8a).
- **Plan or authorization only:** coverage of the occurrence type stays; occurrence NOT_ESTABLISHED, ceiling `OCCURRENCE_NOT_ESTABLISHED`; not MISSING_M (S8b).

### H.2 MISSING_M

MISSING_M requires a complete, reproducible row-by-row exclusion against **every** frozen row's `preciseMeaning` and `explicitNonMeaning`, under modality and scope. This act supplies none.

- **Parking lottery:** unverified in both directions. Rows `M-RES-ROLE-CONTROLLED-ACCESS` and `M-RES-HIERARCHICAL-RETENTION` (CORR4 lines 863–864) were inspected earlier. That was not a sweep of the registry.
- **D-07 Tree consumers:** for example TT-STPSTJ-RS. They do not answer the coverage question.
- `NON_ANALYTICAL` is not missing M [E].
- Reviewer disagreement about membership is a failure to reproduce, not a grammar gap [B].

### H.3 GRAMMAR_GAP

**Negative branch (preserved).** None of the following is a gap; they are design behaviour:

- §F.4.1 L-N insufficiency;
- §G-8 inner states;
- FW-4's epistemic ceiling (`NOT_ESTABLISHED_WITHIN_BOUND` describes the search, not the world);
- §J-3 silence;
- lawful `OPEN`, `NO_LAWFUL_LINKAGE` and `NOT_DETERMINABLE`.

Also:

- D-relevance is not a grammar obligation.
- §H-2 already routes an unmatched proposition to `UNMAPPED_OBSERVATION`.
- An unclear rule is ambiguity of that rule.

**Candidate admission (restored, P line 418) [B].** A candidate grammar insufficiency is admitted only with **all** of:

1. the frozen representational obligation, quoted verbatim with its CORR4 locator;
2. competent evidence for the proposition under the controlling §G;
3. the lawful encodings actually attempted, each listed;
4. the named reason each attempted encoding fails, citing the rule it violates;
5. explicit `ENCODING_COMPLETENESS_NOT_ESTABLISHED`, unless completeness is separately demonstrated.

If any element is missing, the candidate is not admitted and stays NOT_ESTABLISHED (S27). An admitted candidate is at most `SUPPORTED_NOT_CAUSAL` as a schema candidate. It is not an established gap, not a CLC (J-1), and it routes to a separately authorized schema review [E: LOCK §14].

**No positive GRAMMAR_GAP is currently independently verified.** This rubric creates no obligation in order to manufacture one [X], and does not claim the four-category engine is validated.
